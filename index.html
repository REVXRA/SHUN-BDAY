A LITTLE NOTE BEFORE YOU BEGIN · 01

Kya hua? Bhool gaye? 🌸

🐱🌸🐾🎀

Kya hua? Yahi laga na ki shayad iss baar bhool gaye?

Shayad iss baar sach mein maine khamoshi chun li. Shayad main galat hoon, shayad tumne aisa socha hi na ho… aur shayad socha ho. Kise pata?

But here I am, once again, with whatever I can give you, with all that I know and all that I have in me at this moment.

Because I remember.

I remember the things you say, even the ones you might not realise would stay with someone. And I remember you saying that no one would do this for you.

And maybe this little effort won't change everything. Maybe it doesn't have to.

As long as I'm here, as long as I have a mind that remembers and a heart that cares…

I don't want you to feel like there's no one for you.

I can't promise that the whole world will always understand you. I can't promise to fix every difficult day or make every worry disappear.

But I hope I can remind you, even if it's just through something as small as this, that somewhere in this world, there's someone who remembers the little things about you. Someone who listens, someone who cares, someone who wants to see you happy.

Because you do matter. More than you might realise.

And I hope this little surprise reminds you of that.

Now, before you read your birthday wish, there's just one more thing I want you to know.

This might not be the biggest gift you've received. It might not be the most expensive or the most impressive. And perhaps, compared to everything your loved ones have done for you, this little creation of mine might seem like something small.

But this is what these hands of mine could make for you.

I've always felt there's a different kind of happiness in giving someone something that carries a little piece of you. Something you sat down to create, spent your time on, put your thoughts into, and worked hard on because you wanted it to be a little better.

Something that exists because, at some point, you thought of that person and decided they were worth the effort.

That's what this is.

Every little detail, every page, every thought I put into this… they're all little pieces of the time and effort I wanted to give you. Maybe you won't notice every single thing. Maybe you'll never know how much thought went into some of it. And that's okay.

I made it because I wanted you to have something that came from me. Something you could keep, revisit, and perhaps smile at even when this birthday has become just another memory.

So, take your time. Read what's waiting for you. I hope it makes you smile, even if just for a little while.

And when you're done, come back here.

There's one last thing I want to tell you.
